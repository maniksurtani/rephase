One journey.
A continuous rhythm.

Three ways to move through days, flights and changing clocks. The same journey, with a different balance of calm, detail and context.

Scroll inside each phone. Tap an activity or the clock-change banner.Illustrative schedules for layout comparison, not live recommendations.

My recommendation: A, with B’s continuous activity bands.Flow gives the day room to breathe and keeps the next action easy to find. A subtle flight track and a full-width clock-change card make the journey continuous without repeating flight details.
A change of clock, never a break in the journey.The clock handoff is an explicit display choice during the flight. Events remain ordered by elapsed time. A rest period continues through the handoff, even when the local date moves backwards or skips ahead.
