**Escape Lounge, Concourse B** is separate from The Lounge's Terminal B facility. The operator confirms 06:00–21:00, a hot/cold buffet menu and Wi-Fi. It lists Amex Platinum and Delta Reserve on Delta-marketed flights; no Priority Pass entitlement is invented. The current US access page also publishes a US-issued Amex booking requirement. A July 2025 visit, published in October, supports a 3.5/5 assessment: good hot food, friendly welcome and a very small, crowded room.

SJU uses ISO territory code **PR**. Existing route inference may classify mainland-US/PR travel as international because its country codes differ. These records intentionally grant no premium-cabin or airline-alliance rights from that inference; Priority Pass and card routes do not require an international route. Any future airline entitlement must model territory exceptions explicitly. The Lounge Terminal B, transfer-path verification and current reviews are follow-ups.
