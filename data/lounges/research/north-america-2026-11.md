# North American hub starter guides — 18 November 2026

This batch adds **22 selected lounges at 11 airports**, with **21 editorial ratings**. It is a useful initial selection, not a complete lounge inventory or a claim that unlisted access routes are unavailable. All source URLs in these JSON records were opened on 18 November 2026; review dates refer to the actual documented visit where available. Reviews inform quality only. Operational facts come from operators, airports or access programmes.

No images are copied or embedded. Every record has an external photo/article page link; the unrated Houston United Club links to opening coverage, not a purported firsthand review.

## Evidence and policy decisions

### American Express

Opened the [current network access policy](https://www.americanexpress.com/en-us/travel/lounges/centurion-network/access/) and each airport's Amex directory record (exact location URLs are in the JSON). It provides ordinary three-hour entry and a **five-hour connection window**, excluding same-day round trips from the latter. The separate five-hour Platinum rule is explicitly conditional on a qualifying connection. No generic trip context is used to establish it. Delta Reserve routes model either Delta marketing or operation as separate OR alternatives. Final-arrival visits are excluded.

The seven location pages confirm showers and Wi-Fi. They render `InvalidDateTime` for their daily opening periods. Hours remain unknown except DEN, whose [airport location page](https://www.flydenver.com/relax/american-express-centurion-lounge/) confirms 05:00–22:00. No dates or times from search snippets were promoted into opening schedules. Current facilities lists do not establish spa availability or a particular dining service style, so those fields remain unknown.

The [IAH Amex location page](https://www.americanexpress.com/en-us/travel/lounges/the-platinum-card/IAH/The-Centurion-Lounge-Terminal-D-szoGgVnikl/) explicitly warns of expansion works, reduced seating, amenity limitations and occasional short closures. Every IAH Centurion rule therefore asks the traveller to confirm operation during construction. Its 2023 quality review is labelled as a historical baseline.

Potential existing-guide follow-up: SLC's explanatory text still describes connection exceptions without the newly verified five-hour Centurion window. This task does not modify that record. This change applies to the reviewed US network policy, not automatically to every country/card variant.

### American / British Airways

[AA Flagship policy and locations](https://www.aa.com/web/i18n/travel-info/clubs/flagship-lounge.html) confirms ORD 04:15–22:15, DFW 05:00–23:15 and MIA 05:00–22:30. Explicit Qantas/BA/Cathay qualifying status rules are separated from generic alliance status and American/Alaska rules. Qualifying international geography and ticket branding cannot all be resolved from cabin class, so those routes remain conditional. Paid Admirals Club membership is not treated as Flagship access.

[AA's joint JFK lounge page](https://www.aa.com/web/i18n/travel-info/clubs/joint-premium-lounges.html) separates Greenwich above Gate 12 (04:15–01:15) from Soho above Gate 14 (04:15–23:30). Soho's restaurant and rest-area facilities are confirmed; Greenwich's broader meal description does not establish a restaurant service. Generic oneworld status remains conditional where the issuer could be American/Alaska. No Chelsea entitlement is inferred from generic First class.

AA's Flagship Dining eligibility has evolved beyond older descriptions of a First-only product. Records say that it requires a separate qualifying ticket, rather than incorrectly promising it to status visitors or making a stale exhaustive fare list.

### United

United's live lounge access page returned a JavaScript-only shell, and Star Alliance's public policy endpoint returned 403. [Chase's official United Club guide](https://www.chase.com/personal/credit-cards/education/rewards-benefits/guide-to-united-lounges) establishes paid Club membership plus a same-day United/Star Alliance operated flight. The starter records deliberately implement that verifiable membership route only, conditional on a same-calendar-day boarding pass because the matcher cannot establish that for visits before a midnight departure. Generic Star Gold, United Gold/Platinum/1K, ordinary domestic First and Polaris are **not** inferred as automatic access. Contracted non-Star partners, individual/all-access guest differences and premium-ticket/status routes need further directly applicable policy evidence.

[Newark Airport Terminal C](https://www.newarkairport.com/explore-ewr/terminals/terminal-c) confirms C123's showers, buffets and coffee facilities; [United's opening release](https://united.mediaroom.com/2022-05-19-United-Opens-Its-Largest-Club-in-the-Country) confirms Wi-Fi and precise location. Hours could not be verified from a current official location schedule.

[Denver Airport B West](https://www.flydenver.com/relax/united-club-west/) confirms B32, 05:00–21:00, buffet, Wi-Fi and wellness rooms. This is the replacement West lounge, not B44 East. Its December 2025 review is a firsthand visit, not an opening announcement.

[Houston Airports' 21 October release](https://www.fly2houston.com/airport-business/newsroom/press-releases/item/united-airlines-terminal-b-north-opens-at-iah/) confirms the new B North club opened on 22 October 2026, mezzanine position, buffet/Wi-Fi and the distinction from Club Fly. The [older alerts page](https://www.fly2houston.com/iah/alerts/) still discusses closure of the previous club: do not use it to mark the new club closed. No current official hours were established. [AFAR's opening coverage](https://www.afar.com/magazine/united-airliness-massive-houston-lounge-is-now-open) has photos but is not a documented independent visit, so **`iah-united-club-b-north` has no qualityRating**. Do not transfer an old Terminal B review to it.

### Delta and Alaska

Reopened [Delta's network-wide access policy](https://www.delta.com/us/en/delta-sky-club/access), including US Platinum's ten annual visits, Reserve's fifteen, Basic fare exclusions, Delta One distinction and non-Delta SkyTeam issuer exception. ATL uses the same source-supported rules as the existing SLC records and adds a conditional non-Delta international SkyTeam premium route requiring First/Business on every journey segment. No ordinary domestic First or Delta elite-in-Economy automatic rule is introduced. The ordinary three-hour entry limit is conservative for connections; allowance/fee confirmation is required. Amex's [Concourse F directory entry](https://www.americanexpress.com/en-us/travel/lounges/delta-reserve/ATL/Delta-Sky-Club-Concourse-F-P7OlISdHvO/) confirms location, showers and Wi-Fi but its hours are invalid, hence unknown.

[Alaska locations](https://www.alaskaair.com/content/airport-lounge/location-and-hours), [amenities](https://www.alaskaair.com/content/airport-lounge/perks-and-amenities) and [access rules](https://www.alaskaair.com/content/airport-lounge/policies-and-rules) confirm the N lounge and 05:00–23:00. The current policy uses a **2,000-mile** flight threshold for its eligible paid/award First entitlement, not the 2,100-mile threshold in older reviews. Upgrades are different. Eligible status must be on the reservation before check-in. Non-US alliance programmes, Atmos international geography and Admirals Club partner eligibility are separate; Single memberships/supplementary card users cannot silently establish partner admission. American-status international geography and non-oneworld partner routes remain incomplete. The policy prohibits sleeping/lying across furniture; the lounge is not advertised as a sleep facility.

Signature Suite is not part of these access rules. Air Canada's detailed hours page did not load. YVR quiet-area/snack/Wi-Fi facts are confirmed there. YYZ facilities not independently established by a current official location source remain unknown.

Plaza Premium operators and Priority Pass location pages were opened for [YYZ T3](https://www.plazapremiumlounge.com/en-uk/find/americas/canada/toronto/toronto-pearson-international-airport/international-departures-terminal-three) and [YVR Pier D](https://www.plazapremiumlounge.com/en-uk/find/americas/canada/vancouver/vancouver-international-airport/international-departures-pier-d-gate-67). Airline/other-status routes need further policy evidence; Priority Pass remains conditional because its daily admission blackout cannot be encoded as a simple pre-departure duration.
