## Access and operating evidence

### Boston

[Priority Pass's The Lounge record](https://www.prioritypass.com/en-GB/lounges/united-states-of-america/logan-international/bos10-the-lounge) has different closing times: 22:30 on Monday/Wednesday/Saturday, 22:00 otherwise. The day-by-day schedule takes precedence over the simplified headline. Access starts three hours before departure, with a three-hour stay. Gates C40–C42 cannot use the lounge; space restrictions are highlighted from 14:30–20:00. [Amex's page](https://www.americanexpress.com/en-us/travel/lounges/the-platinum-card/BOS/The-Lounge-BOS-Terminal-C-lBn0xltjPl/) additionally says Terminal C departures only. The record preserves the gate/access uncertainty as a condition rather than declaring other terminals eligible.

[Delta's Boston media kit](https://news.delta.com/mediakit/boston-logan-international-airport) verifies the Terminal E Sky Club's showers and private phone booths. [Amex](https://www.americanexpress.com/en-us/travel/lounges/delta-reserve/BOS/Delta-Sky-Club-Terminal-E-xCaCyeurbb/) confirms E13, showers and Wi-Fi. Both the Delta dynamic directory and Amex hours failed to provide usable current fixed opening times (the latter rendered `InvalidDateTime`); no timetable was copied from a review. This is the regular Sky Club, not the adjacent Delta One restaurant lounge. Structured dining remains unknown because current operator text does not specify buffet versus restaurant service.

### Washington Dulles

[United's 9 November announcement](https://united.mediaroom.com/2026-11-09-United-Airlines-and-the-Metropolitan-Washington-Airports-Authority-Unveil-First-New-Concourse-in-Two-Decades-at-Washington-Dulles-International-Airport) verifies the Concourse E Club opened 10 November, opposite E14/E16, with daily 05:30–22:00 hours, buffet stations, phone rooms and Wi-Fi. [Chase's United Club policy](https://www.chase.com/personal/credit-cards/education/rewards-benefits/guide-to-united-lounges) supports the paid-membership route with same-day United/Star travel. United's current lounge-access page timed out and the Star Alliance policy page returned 403; status/premium/day-pass access was not inferred from reviews.

[Priority Pass](https://www.prioritypass.com/en-GB/lounges/united-states-of-america/washington-dulles-international/iad11-turkish-airlines-lounge-washington) publishes Turkish's daily hours of 07:15–21:30, three-hour stay and same-day IAD outbound pass requirement. [Amex](https://www.americanexpress.com/en-us/travel/lounges/the-platinum-card/IAD/Turkish-Airlines-Lounge-Washington-D-C-Concourse-B-4pPs59HlBj/) verifies buffet dining, showers and Wi-Fi. [ANA](https://www.ana.co.jp/en/us/travel-information/lounges/washington-dc/) documents international Star premium/Gold eligibility, which is encoded separately from the PP route. Unlike DTW Lufthansa, the current PP page gives ordinary opening hours without an explicit membership-only admission restriction. The hours source is stated; domestic and contracted-airline exceptions remain follow-ups.

The [airport's official directory](https://www.flydulles.com/travel-information/services-amenities/airport-lounges) explicitly marks Lufthansa temporarily closed for renovation. It was not added as an open alternative. The airport page still uses some legacy partner descriptions, so it is not used to infer current alliance membership.

### Charlotte

[American's location page](https://www.aa.com/web/i18n/travelInformation/airportAmenities/clt-club.html) verifies Admirals B between B3 and B5, daily 05:15–22:45 and Wi-Fi. Current food service style and showers are not specified; absence from the facilities list is not proof of absence. [American's access policy](https://www.aa.com/web/i18n/travel-info/clubs/admirals-club-access.html) distinguishes paid membership, qualifying premium routes and status issuer. Qantas/BA/Cathay elite rules require both oneworld marketing and operating carriers; generic alliance status remains conditional because AAdvantage and Atmos restrictions differ. AA international premium/elite rules additionally require confirmation that the route qualifies, avoiding automatic eligibility for US territory routes based only on differing country codes. Ordinary domestic First alone is insufficient.

[Priority Pass](https://www.prioritypass.com/en-GB/lounges/united-states-of-america/charlotte-douglas-international/clt5-the-club-clt) and [The Club's own location page](https://theclubairportlounges.com/lounges/clt-the-club-main-terminal) both restrict PP/LoungeKey entry to **150 minutes before departure**, versus 180 minutes for a purchased Club Pass. PP also caps the stay at 150 minutes and flags 14:00–18:30 capacity pressure. The operator verifies showers with a USD20 charge, work/quiet areas and Wi-Fi. The current operator wording establishes food but not service style, so structured dining remains unknown.

## Quality evidence

Each JSON links its own independently read review:

- BOS Sky Club E: [One Mile at a Time](https://onemileatatime.com/reviews/delta-sky-club-boston/), November 2025 visit published in 2026; attractive interiors, food/showers and quiet early-morning experience, with time-of-day caveat.
- BOS The Lounge: [Points with a Crew](https://www.pointswithacrew.com/the-lounge-bos-lounge-review-boston-logan-airport-priority-pass/), January 2026; basic provisions and weak paid-entry value.
- IAD United E: [From the Tray Table](https://fromthetraytable.com/united-club-dulles-concourse-e/), November 2026 opening preview; strong design, ordinary buffet and limited laptop-friendly seating. The score is explicitly provisional opening evidence, not an ordinary busy-day experience.
- IAD Turkish: [Your Mileage May Vary](https://yourmileagemayvary.com/2026/05/26/turkish-airlines-lounge-washington-dulles-review/), May 2026; good food, crowding and awkward table space.
- CLT Admirals B: [The Bulkhead Seat](https://thebulkheadseat.com/lounge-review-admirals-club-clt-concourse-b/), August 2026; small but pleasant room, friendly staff and views. Reviewer did not taste the breakfast, which is disclosed.
- CLT The Club: [Explore with Intent](https://explorewithintent.com/theclubclt/), 2026 account; friendly staff but limited food, noise and admission waits.
- DTW Lufthansa: [BoardingArea](https://boardingarea.com/review-lufthansa-lounge-detroit-international-dtw-evans-terminal/), October 2026 account of a late-September visit; dated but useful, adequate catering and work facilities.
- DTW A38: [Live and Let's Fly](https://liveandletsfly.com/delta-sky-cub-review-detroit-a38/), March 2022 historical review (later page update is not a new visit); generous food but worn decor and crowds. Explicitly label the old evidence and prioritize re-review.
- MSP G17/G18: [Empty Nester Miles](https://emptynestermiles.com/2025/09/22/delta-skyclub-minneapolis-g18-review/), September 2025; varied buffet, spacious seating and Sky Deck, with a long walk and time-specific crowding caveat.
- MSP Escape: [Upgraded Points](https://upgradedpoints.com/travel/airports/escape-lounge-minneapolis-st-paul-msp-review/), 2026 publication; tasty limited food, worn furnishings and seating pressure. Its operational claims were not imported as current amenities.
