# Chengdu Tianfu (TFU) research — 20 November 2026

Added four selected lounges in `TFU.json`: Air China Level 4, Air China Level 2, China Eastern V2 and the T1 International First and Business Class Lounge. This is **Tianfu (TFU)**, not **Shuangliu (CTU)**. The airport catalog supplies `Asia/Shanghai`.

All operational and quality sources below were opened on 20 November 2026. A checked date is not a new visit date. No photographs were copied, downloaded into the app or embedded: the three photo links open the original reviews.

## Primary evidence and access boundaries

### Air China: two separate domestic sections

Read the [simplified-Chinese Chengdu directory](https://webresource.airchina.com.cn/zh-CN/content/travel_info/airport_guide/vip_lounge/chengdu/) and [traditional-Chinese directory](https://webresource.airchina.com.cn/zh-TW/content/travel_info/airport_guide/vip_lounge/chengdu/). Text extraction exposed only the lounge headings, so I fetched the public HTML and read the `content-data` attributes for the individual Tianfu panels. The Shuangliu panels were excluded.

The pages identify separate upper Level 4 and lower Level 2 spaces, reached after premium security checkpoint 1. Current facilities confirm showers, Wi-Fi and hot food; they do not establish dining service style, a staffed spa or a dedicated quiet area. Those fields remain unknown. The [Air China November 2025 catering notice](https://pur.airchina.com.cn/001/001001/20251113/b297c703-2e88-4c01-a9d5-827abf098443.html) corroborates Terminal 2.

The simplified-Chinese panels publish 04:55 until the last flight finishes; the traditional-Chinese version opens two hours before the first flight and closes after final boarding. Both records remain flight-dependent, with the conflicting opening descriptions visible. A guessed closing time is not encoded.

The [official access matrix](https://m.airchina.com.cn/c/invoke/zyxxsRules@pg) distinguishes own member/mixed lounges from separate First-and-Business lounges. Star Alliance Gold on a Star-operated domestic departure belongs in the member section; it does not establish access to the separate premium section. Business/First on Star-operated departures is represented for both. The traditional-Chinese directory explicitly identifies the lower premium section, while the simplified-Chinese page uses the same broad Premium Lounge label for both floors. Consequently the Level 2 rule also requires confirming the assigned section on the boarding pass/check-in invitation. No PhoenixMiles tiers or paid-pass products were invented to fill gaps in existing membership IDs.

### China Eastern V2: airline-restricted Priority Pass

[Priority Pass](https://www.prioritypass.com/en-GB/lounges/china/chengdu-tianfu-international/tfu2-china-eastern-airlines-lounge) confirms domestic T2 access, a two-hour visit limit, and eligibility only while flying MU, FM or KN. These restrictions are predicates, not merely notes. The lounge is opposite Gate 214; it is not the adjoining Sichuan Airlines lounge. Complimentary daybeds support the quiet/rest amenity.

[Plaza Premium's exact V2 partner page](https://www.plazapremiumlounge.com/en-uk/find/china-regions/mainland-china/chengdu/chengdu-tianfu-international-airport/v2-domestic-departure) verifies Wi-Fi and food/beverages, but not a dining service style or showers. Priority Pass opens at 06:00 until the last China Eastern flight; Plaza Premium starts at 05:30. The membership-specific 06:00 rule is retained and the discrepancy documented. No unlimited opening hours or generic Plaza Premium membership entitlement is inferred from the booking page.

### T1 international contract lounge: narrowly scoped invitations

[Singapore Airlines' partner directory](https://www.singaporeair.com/en_UK/sg/flying-withus/before-the-flight/lounges/partner/) identifies the T1 Level 3 lounge beside Gate 117 and confirms showers, hot food/snacks and Wi-Fi. Its linked [partner-access PDF](https://www.singaporeair.com/content/dam/sia/web-assets/pdfs/flying-withus/lounge/Partner-Lounge-Access.pdf) sends third-party lounge customers to the invitation on their boarding pass or check-in assignment. Both encoded SQ-operated international rules—premium cabin and Star Gold—therefore retain that confirmation as a material condition. Existing program expansion lets KrisFlyer Elite Gold and United Premier Gold/Platinum/1K reach the generic Star Gold rule.

[Asiana's TFU airport page](https://flyasiana.com/C/SG/EN/travel/airport?airportCd=TFU) corroborates the location, showers, Wi-Fi and flight-dependent operation. [EVA's mainland-China lounge directory](https://www.evaair.com/en-sg/fly-prepare/at-the-airport/lounges/partner-lounges/mainland-china/) separates TFU from CTU and lists 07:00–22:30 subject to flights. This is not stored as a guaranteed fixed weekly schedule. No automatic Priority Pass access or universal Star Alliance entitlement is asserted. Asiana/EVA location listings alone do not establish their complete admission rules, so their invitations are not yet encoded.

## Useful regression checks

- Priority Pass + MU/FM/KN domestic: V2 matches, capped at 120 minutes. CA operating an MU-marketed flight must not match V2; neither may international departures.
- Star Gold + CA domestic: Level 4 matches; the separate Level 2 cabin rule does not. Premium cabin + CA domestic: Level 2 is conditional on assignment.
- Star Gold or Business + SQ international: T1 is conditional on the assigned invitation. SQ-marketed Scoot operation must not qualify.
- Domestic status access does not spill into the T1 international lounge, and CTU does not borrow TFU records.
- All four opening records stay uncertain/flight-dependent instead of claiming a known open or closed interval from guessed schedules.
