## Primary evidence and conflicts

- [China Southern English PKX facilities](https://www.csair.com/sg/en/tourguide/airport_service/priority_pass/domestic/1e1164lke811s.shtml) separates the three domestic lounges from the international lounge. Its [Chinese version](https://www.csair.cn/cn/tourguide/airport_service/priority_pass/domestic/1dpdb6s1n18bh.shtml) groups many domestic amenities, so that combined list was not assigned wholesale to Lounge 1. The international subsection is specific to the international facility.
- [Plaza Premium's No. 1 listing](https://www.plazapremiumlounge.com/en-uk/find/china-regions/mainland-china/beijing/beijing-daxing-international-airport/china-southern-airlines-no1-vip-lou) confirms the domestic buffet and separately flags shower charges/availability. Its paid two-hour package does not establish Priority Pass admission or limit airline-entitled visits.
- The [international China Southern partner listing](https://www.plazapremiumlounge.com/en-uk/find/china-regions/mainland-china/beijing/beijing-daxing-international-airport/china-southern-lounge) opens at 06:30, versus 06:00 on the airline page. Both tie closing to flights. Keep `flight-dependent` with the discrepancy visible, not a fabricated fixed closing time.
- [Priority Pass CHUM](https://www.prioritypass.com/en-GB/lounges/china/beijing-daxing-international/pkx4-chum-vip-lounge) places the lounge near A100–A105; [Plaza's CHUM Domestic](https://www.plazapremiumlounge.com/en-uk/find/china-regions/mainland-china/beijing/beijing-daxing-international-airport/chumdom) instead says B20. Whether this is a relocation, renumbering or different facility is unresolved. Only the directly verified Priority Pass record is included; Plaza's amenities and the ambiguous review are not merged into it.
- [Priority Pass East Pacific](https://www.prioritypass.com/en-GB/lounges/china/beijing-daxing-international/pkx-east-pacific-passenger-lounge) shows 00:00–23:59 daily, while the [booking partner's exact page](https://www.plazapremiumlounge.com/en-uk/find/china-regions/mainland-china/beijing/beijing-daxing-international-airport/east-pacific-passenger-lounge-international-depart) explicitly says 24 hours. The structured period is therefore 00:00–24:00, without a fictitious one-minute closure. Priority Pass's three-hour allowance is not replaced by the partner's different two-hour paid package.

Massage chairs/areas are not evidence of a staffed spa. An ordinary seating area or a claim about a calm atmosphere is not evidence of a dedicated quiet zone. General Food & Beverage labels do not establish a buffet or restaurant. Reviews supply quality assessments, not current operating facts or access rights.
