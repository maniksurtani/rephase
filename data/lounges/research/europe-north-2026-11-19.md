# Northern and western Europe lounge expansion

Checked **2026-11-19**. Added selected useful lounges at ARN (2), OSL (3), BRU (3), and LIS (3): **11 lounges, 9 independently rated**. These are selected guides, not complete airport inventories. Source URLs and check dates are stored alongside each lounge. No images were copied or hotlinked; photo links open the publisher's review page.

## ARN — Stockholm Arlanda

- **SAS Lounge E1**: the current combined lounge, not separate historical Business/Gold records. [SAS](https://www.flysas.com/en/travel-info/at-the-airport/lounges) and [Swedavia](https://www.swedavia.com/arlanda/service/sas-business-scandinavian2/) verify E1, showers and current access. SAS is SkyTeam; an old Star Alliance status rule would be wrong. Domestic Premium is a separate qualifying product, not a blanket long-haul Premium Economy entitlement. Main-lounge hours depend on SAS departures.
- **Aspire non-Schengen**: [the operator](https://www.aspirelounges.com/airport-lounges/stockholm-arlanda-airport-aspire-lounge-non-schengen/) supplies current amenities and hours. [Priority Pass](https://www.prioritypass.com/en-GB/lounges/sweden/stockholm-arlanda/arn9-aspire-lounge-non-schengen) verifies membership access, F-pier location and entry from three hours before departure. The operator closes earlier than the Priority Pass listing (20:00 weekdays / 21:30 weekends versus 22:00 daily); use operator hours and retain a confirmation condition. Access does not promise separate paid Luxe/Suite upgrades. Shower/premium-drink surcharges are noted without inventing prices.

Quality: [TravelUpdate, June 2026](https://travelupdate.com/review-sas-lounge-stockholm-arn/) was read for the combined SAS facility (3.5/5). Aspire remains unrated: a current relevant review could not be opened; Schengen Aspire or paid premium-section reviews cannot substitute.

Further coverage: Pearl C37, other-terminal Pearl lounges, Schengen Aspire and Amex by Pontus. Resolve contradictory Pearl C37 passport-control metadata before adding route rules.

## OSL — Oslo Gardermoen

[SAS](https://www.flysas.com/en/travel-info/at-the-airport/lounges) and [Avinor](https://www.avinor.no/en/airport/oslo/info/lounge/) were read for the two SAS lounges and independent OSL Lounge International.

- SAS Domestic is by A2; SAS International is upstairs near E2 before passport control. Domestic and international route rules prevent recommending the wrong security zone. Domestic showers are verified; international showers remain unknown rather than borrowed from the domestic listing.
- OSL Lounge International includes selected documented airline Business invitations and **Amex Platinum issued in Norway, Sweden or Finland**. The country-of-issuance restriction remains a condition because preferences do not capture it. The separate Premium section is not treated as a universal entitlement. No Priority Pass participation was established.
- Reception purchase is described in the note (NOK 495, subject to availability, from three hours before an international departure). It is deliberately not an access rule: a cabin alone does not represent having purchased lounge access.

Quality: [Quiet Vacationer, December 2025](https://quietvacationer.com/2025/12/10/review-sas-domestic-lounge-oslo-osl/) supports Domestic 3.5/5; [Verylvke, October 2026](https://www.verylvke.com/en/2026/10/11/my-review-of-sas-schengen-lounge-oslo/) supports International 3/5. The [2022 independent OSL Lounge review](https://callumelsdon.com/2022/05/30/snapshot-review-osl-lounge-oslo-gardermoen-airport/) is too old and affected by Premium-area closure to rate today's section; it is labelled as a historical photo link. A 2026-published SAS review describing a pre-alliance-change visit was not used as current evidence.

Further coverage: independent domestic OSL lounge, Premium-area-specific eligibility, and membership schemes not currently in the supported catalog.

## BRU — Brussels

The [airline's current access page](https://www.brusselsairlines.com/be/en/extra-services/lounges/brussels-airport-lounges), [airport airline-lounge page](https://www.brusselsairport.be/en/passenger/at-the-airport/services/lounges/brussels-airlines-lounges) and [Pop-Up announcement](https://press.brusselsairlines.com/brussels-airlines-opens-pop-up-lounge-for-premium-passengers-during-the-loft-renovation) establish that **THE LOFT is closed**, with a smaller temporary replacement at A27, level -1. Do not copy the closed flagship's showers, nap rooms or rating to the pop-up. Holiday exceptions for December 24/25/31 and January 1 are encoded from the airport page. Older reopening targets conflict, so none is promised.

The web text extraction drops accordion headings on the airline access page. Raw HTML was successfully fetched and inspected on the check date: `maui-collapsible-item` has explicit `headline` attributes associating the paragraphs with Business, HON Circle, Senator, Frequent Traveller and Star Alliance Gold. This resolves the initially ambiguous status evidence:

- Pop-Up Business eligibility names SN, LH, LX, OS, AZ and LO, not every Star Alliance Business ticket.
- Pop-Up Star Gold and Miles & More Frequent Traveller have separate Star-carrier access. Existing alliance mapping includes AZ. Senator/HON status expands to Star Gold in the model.
- At **The View**, generic Star Gold requires an **SN-operated flight**. Senator/HON has a broader Star-carrier route with a physical/digital Miles & More card condition. Frequent Traveller is expressly excluded there.
- Airline instructions suspend paid entry while THE LOFT is closed, overriding broader airport-page prices.

[Airport lounge listings](https://www.brusselsairport.be/en/passenger/at-the-airport/services/lounges/brussels-airport-lounges/) and [Priority Pass The View](https://www.prioritypass.com/en-GB/lounges/belgium/brussels-zaventem/bru3-the-view-lounge) / [Diamond](https://www.prioritypass.com/en-GB/lounges/belgium/brussels-zaventem/bru1-diamond-lounge) verify the independent alternatives. The View is post-passport B; Diamond A is Schengen by A42. Both PP entry windows and visit limits are three hours. Diamond's inconsistent PP passport metadata is not repeated. Unknown facility fields stay unknown; review photos alone do not establish current operational amenities.

Quality: [The Travel Happiness, November 5, 2026 visit](https://thetravelhappiness.com/brussels-airlines-business-class-kurzstrecke/) supports Pop-Up 2.5/5, explicitly rating its lounge section rather than the flight; [Trip by Trip, 2026](https://tripbytrip.org/2026/09/20/review-the-view-lounge-brussels-airport-b-gates-extra-schengen/) supports The View 3.5/5; [Last Man Boarding, August 2025](https://lastmanboarding.ch/2025/08/21/new-review-diamond-lounge-bruessel-bru/) supports Diamond 3/5. ## LIS — Lisbon

[TAP's lounge directory](https://www.flytap.com/en-at/manage-booking/extras/lounges) verifies **Tejo** before passport control and **Atlântico** after passport control, both in Terminal 1, 05:00–24:00. TAP presently reports Tejo showers/restrooms closed for renovation; showers are therefore unavailable rather than inherited from a historical review. TAP Business and Star Gold on TP-operated flights are the verified routes modeled here. Partner-carrier and paid-access rules require further fare-specific verification; no blanket Star Business or Premium Economy entitlement is assumed. TAP describes hot/cold meals without unambiguously classifying the current dining service, so dining remains unknown with a descriptive note.

[ANA's lounge page](https://www.lisbonairport.pt/en/lis/services-shopping/premium-services/ana-lounge?language=en), [Priority Pass](https://www.prioritypass.com/en-GB/lounges/portugal/lisbon-portela/lis6-ana-lounge-lisbon), and [ANA's April 2026 rules PDF](https://www.ana.pt/sites/default/files/2026-04/loungeana_rulesofuse_LIS.pdf) establish ANA Lounge in T1, level 6, before passport control, usable before either departure zone. Current hours are 04:00–23:00; showers/rest booths and light refreshments are verified. The PDF allows entry from **3h30 before boarding**, with a maximum three-hour visit. Boarding is not departure: retain that entry requirement as a condition rather than incorrectly setting a departure-based timer. Possible Christmas closures require confirmation, not guessed dated exceptions.

Quality: [Frugal Flyer, 2025 trip / 2026 publication](https://frugalflyer.ca/blog/review-tap-air-portugal-business-class-a330-900neo/) supports Tejo 3.5/5, with an explicit note that it predates the shower closure. ## Integration and regression cases

Required country mapping: **ARN→SE, OSL→NO, BRU→BE, LIS→PT**. Existing SK→SkyTeam and SN/TP/AZ→Star mappings are appropriate. No new membership IDs, schema changes or image assets are required. Generic SkyTeam Elite Plus is available for SAS EuroBonus users until that program has its own profile entry.

Useful boundary cases:

1. Star Gold flying SK does not qualify for SAS lounges; SkyTeam Elite Plus does, subject to the documented scheduled-flight restriction.
2. OSL domestic SAS and international SAS records must not match the opposite route. Amex outside the three named issuing countries is not an unconditional OSL entitlement.
3. United 1K on a Star carrier can match Pop-Up Loft for a Schengen departure; Business without status on an unlisted Star carrier cannot use its cabin rule.
4. Generic Star Gold on a non-SN international flight does not match The View's Star Gold rule; Miles & More Senator has a distinct broader rule. Priority Pass remains independent.
5. LIS Atlântico and ARN Aspire non-Schengen do not match Schengen departures; ANA before passport control is usable for both route zones.
6. Tejo cannot satisfy a shower priority as though its showers were open; historical review photographs do not override this closure.
7. ANA's boarding-based entry window must not be converted into a departure-based three-hour rule.
