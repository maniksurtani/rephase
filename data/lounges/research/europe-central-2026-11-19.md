# VIE, WAW, LGW and DUB research — 19 November 2026

Four airport guides, 12 selected lounges: VIE 4, WAW 3, LGW 3, DUB 2. This is useful initial coverage rather than an exhaustive directory. Primary pages and the independent reviews cited below were opened and read on 2026-11-19. Search snippets were used for discovery only. No photographs were downloaded: the guides link to review pages containing photographs.

Ten facilities have an editorial quality score. The two unrated facilities and reasons are in `quality-backlog.json` for integration into the shared quality backlog. Review publication/visit dates are distinguished from our source-check date.

## Vienna (VIE)

Included Austrian Business F, Austrian Senator F, the current combined Austrian non-Schengen G lounge, and independent VIENNA Lounge.

- [Austrian's current guide](https://www.austrian.com/us/en/lounges-vienna-airport) establishes the current G layout and the separation of Business, Senator and HON spaces at F. Its current hours take precedence over the [airport's older Austrian summary](https://viennaairport.com/en/passengers/airport/lounges/austrian_lounges_1), which directs readers to Austrian for updates.
- [Aegean's airline lounge directory](https://en.aegeanair.com/travel-info/travelling-with-aegean/at-the-airport/lounge-finder/?AirlineCode=OS&AirportCode=VIE&CabinClass=B&FFPStatus=&FrequentFlyer=&PaymentMembershipCard=&ShowMore=false) independently confirms current Business F / G locations, hours and showers. Its Business filter does not establish Senator showers; those remain unknown. Senator and G food service style also remains unknown.
- [Vienna Airport's VIENNA Lounge page](https://viennaairport.com/en/passengers/airport/lounges/vienna_lounge) confirms showers, rest loungers, terrace, Priority Pass, an entry window of 3.5 hours before departure and a three-hour visit. [Priority Pass](https://www.prioritypass.com/en-GB/lounges/austria/vienna-international/vie7-vienna-lounge) confirms the facility, hours and F/G shuttle requirement. Its food service style is not inferred from old reviews.

Access separates Schengen F from non-Schengen G using destination countries. This conservatively represents ordinary gate-area use; possible visits to F before another departure require separate confirmation. Business tickets and Frequent Traveller alone do not open the Senator room. Industry-discount exclusions remain conditions. The combined G facility does not inherit an old room's rating.

## Warsaw (WAW)

Included LOT Polonez (Schengen), LOT Mazurek (non-Schengen), and Preludium (independent Schengen).

- [LOT admission rules](https://www.lot.com/pl/en/legal-documents/rules-of-entry-to-lot-business-lounges) are the authority for both LOT rooms. Frequent Traveller access is limited to LO/LH/OS/SN/LX/OU operations, not every Star airline. Charter restrictions remain explicit conditions. The usual four-hour cap has a qualifying-connection exception, recorded for reception confirmation because the current matcher cannot evaluate it.
- [LOT Polonez](https://www.lot.com/pl/en/journey/additional-services/executive-lounges/lot-business-lounge-polonez) confirms Level +1, 05:00–23:00, buffet, showers, relaxation areas, Wi-Fi and spa. The separate Gold area is not represented as generally available. [LOT Mazurek](https://www.lot.com/pl/en/journey/additional-services/executive-lounges/lot-business-lounge-mazurek) confirms Level 0 after passport control, 06:00–23:00, showers, buffet and relaxation areas; spa remains unknown.
- [Priority Pass Preludium](https://www.prioritypass.com/en-GB/lounges/poland/warsaw-chopin/waw3-preludium-lounge) establishes program access and the three-hour entry window. [DragonPass's exact facility record](https://en.dragonpass.com.cn/lounge/329291) confirms showers, Wi-Fi, snacks, Schengen-only use and the Level 0 location. Its separate membership/visit terms were not copied into the Priority Pass rule. Current dining is recorded as snacks; an old review's buffet does not establish today's offering.

## London Gatwick (LGW)

Included British Airways Club South and separate No1 North/South records.

- [BA's Gatwick guide](https://www.britishairways.com/content/en/at/information/airport-information/london-gatwick) confirms the South lounge location and separate First room. [BA's Business lounge policy](https://www.britishairways.com/content/en/bb/information/lounges/business) establishes BA premium-cabin, BA Silver/Gold and oneworld Sapphire/Emerald access. Current fixed hours, showers and food service style were not verified, so remain unknown rather than being filled from old reviews.
- [Gatwick Airport's No1 page](https://www.gatwickairport.com/premium-services/lounge-airport/lounge-no1.html) confirms the two terminals, buffet, Wi-Fi, library and ordinary hours. The library has an over-12 restriction; the lounges are not adult-only.
- [Priority Pass North](https://www.prioritypass.com/en-GB/lounges/united-kingdom/gatwick/lgw15-no1-lounge-gatwick) and [South](https://www.prioritypass.com/en-GB/lounges/united-kingdom/gatwick/lgw17-no1-lounge-gatwick) establish separate access and Christmas schedules. North ordinarily closes at 20:00, South 21:00. Published Christmas hours are recorded for 2026-12-25 only; they are not guessed as permanent annual exceptions.
- [No1's operator FAQ](https://no1lounges.com/faqs/) explicitly states no showers currently in use, overriding older accounts implying showers. Optional paid reservations are not represented as a compulsory entry fee.

## Dublin (DUB)

Included independent Liffey Lounge and premium-cabin 51st & Green after US Preclearance. These cover different sides of the immigration boundary and are not interchangeable.

- [Dublin Airport's Liffey page](https://www.dublinairport.com/enhance-your-journey/lounges/the-liffey-lounge) confirms the T1/T2 connecting corridor, before US Preclearance, cold buffet, showers, quiet workstations and two-hour stay. [Priority Pass](https://www.prioritypass.com/en-GB/lounges/ireland/dublin/dub-liffey-lounge) instead lists three hours, warns of peak restrictions and lists Christmas closure. The guide conservatively allows two hours and flags the unresolved duration difference in its access condition.
- [Dublin Airport's 51st & Green page](https://www.dublinairport.com/enhance-your-journey/lounges/us-preclearance-lounge-51st-green) confirms the post-preclearance position, 07:15–17:00, two-hour stay, buffet, showers and quiet workstations. The [airport's airline partner page](https://www.dublinairport.com/enhance-your-journey/lounges/lounge-airline-partners) specifically names First/Business passengers of Aer Lingus, American, Delta and United, subject to airline terms. Thus the rule requires US destination, named operator and premium cabin, with an invitation/fare check; neither Priority Pass nor alliance status alone is assumed.
