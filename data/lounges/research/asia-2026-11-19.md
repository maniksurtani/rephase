## Operational evidence and decisions

### Kansai

- [ANA's current Kansai lounge page](https://www.ana.co.jp/en/jp/guide/lounge/international/kix/) distinguishes KIX Lounge Kansai from Premium. The former ANA Lounge closed in May 2025. NH Business and Star Alliance Gold on NH are modeled for Kansai, not Premium. Kansai's advertised hours depend on security/flight operations, so the JSON uses `flight-dependent` rather than an unconditional fixed schedule. ANA also supplies Premium's 06:30–00:30 hours.
- [JAL's Kansai page](https://www.jal.co.jp/jp/en/inter/service/lounge/kix/) now identifies KIX Lounge Premium and permits JAL First, Business and Premium Economy. These are modeled only on JL-operated international departures. Other airline invitations, JAL fare-specific Economy access and status-specific routes need separate research; they are not inferred from a generic alliance label.
- The airport's detailed KIX lounge page returned HTTP 403. Search indexing appeared to expose facilities, but that is not treated as an actual page read. Detailed amenities for the two KIX airline lounges therefore remain unknown, even where independent reviews mention facilities.
- [Priority Pass NODOKA](https://www.prioritypass.com/en-GB/lounges/japan/kansai-international/kix12-airport-cafe-lounge-nodoka) establishes the landside Aeroplaza location, three-hour allowance and departure within twelve hours. [NODOKA's own operator page](https://aprecio.jp/nodoka/) confirms 24-hour opening, showers, Wi-Fi, light meals and quiet reclining/mat areas. Its explicit 24-hour service supports `close: "24:00"`, avoiding an artificial one-minute closure from the membership directory's 23:59 convention. The operator's twelve-hour repeat-visit exclusion is represented as a condition. The discounted longer package is not modeled as free admission.

### Ho Chi Minh City

- [Vietnam Airlines' T3 announcement](https://enews.vietnamairlines.com/2025/05/lotus-lounge-terminal-t3-tan-son-nhat-airport/) distinguishes the ordinary Lotus Lounge opposite Gate 10 from Lotus Premier opposite Gate 13. It confirms a buffet, showers, Wi-Fi and separate relaxation space. Fixed hours are not published there and remain unknown.
- [The airline's Lotus access policy](https://www.vietnamairlines.com/us/en/travel-information/airports-transit/lotus-lounge) establishes VN Business eligibility. Its SkyTeam Elite Plus domestic connection requirement cannot be derived from the single-flight query, so the rule remains conditional on a qualifying connection in Vietnam within 24 hours. Lotusmiles Gold and Platinum are not yet selectable program tiers.
- [Priority Pass currently lists Lotus Premier T3](https://www.prioritypass.com/en-GB/lounges/vietnam/tan-son-nhat-international/sgn10-vietnam-airlines-lotus-premier-lounge-sgn-dom-t3), but it is a separate facility. That listing does not grant Priority Pass access to the ordinary Lotus record. Premier could be added in a later expansion after reconciling its membership and airline routes and finding a current exact-facility review.
- [Rose's Priority Pass listing](https://www.prioritypass.com/en-GB/lounges/vietnam/tan-son-nhat-international/sgn5-rose-business-lounge) confirms T2 international departures and a three-hour visit. [SASCO's own Rose page](https://www.sasco.com.vn/en/products/rose-business-lounge-2128) supplies 24-hour opening, buffet, Wi-Fi and a spa area. Treatment inclusions and shower availability are not established. The current rating uses a visit after the December 2025 renovation.

### Hanoi

- [Vietnam Airlines' Lotus policy](https://www.vietnamairlines.com/us/en/travel-information/airports-transit/lotus-lounge) supports international SkyTeam premium cabin and Elite Plus rules and the basic buffet/Wi-Fi offering. [Hainan's official lounge directory](https://www.hainanairlines.com/HUPortal/dyn/portal/DisplayPage?COUNTRY_SITE=VN&LANGUAGE=GB&PAGE=AIST&SITE=CBHZCBHZ) places the international Lotus lounge on the fourth floor via Gates 28–29. Hainan's flight-specific service window is not reused as the entire lounge's hours. Showers and fixed hours remain unverified.
- A [NASCO announcement concerning Lotus T2](https://nasco.com.vn/tin-tuc/su-kien/khai-truong-lotus-lounge-t2-phong-cho-lon-bac-nhat-noi-bai.html) could not be retrieved. Its search result is a follow-up lead, not evidence for a replacement facility or its amenities. Do not assign an older review before confirming it describes the current lounge.
- [Priority Pass's SH Premium T2 East listing](https://www.prioritypass.com/en-GB/lounges/vietnam/noi-bai-international/han3-sh-premium-lounge-ha-noi) sets a three-hour limit and 06:30–02:00 entry hours. [SH's T2 operator page](https://shairportlounge.com.vn/en/sh-premium-lounge-ha-noi.html) confirms buffet, Wi-Fi and showers but gives 06:00 opening. The record uses the applicable membership window. Operator Gate 28 directions differ from the directory's Gates 21–22; this is visible in the record rather than silently picking one. Both identify T2 East Wing.
- [American Express's exact T2 East facility entry](https://www.americanexpress.com/en-in/travel/lounges/the-platinum-card/HAN/Song-Hong-Premium-Lounge-and-Bar-International-Terminal-lfI066JPot/) corroborates location and showers. Its India-specific card page does not establish worldwide Amex eligibility. No global Amex rule was added. Domestic SH and SH Premium Ha Noi 3 are separate facilities.

### Bali

- [Priority Pass Concordia International](https://www.prioritypass.com/en-GB/lounges/indonesia/ngurah-rai-international/dps9-concordia-lounge) verifies international third-floor access and a quiet nap room. It currently states no maximum visit length; one is not invented.
- [Plaza Premium's exact international Concordia partner listing](https://www.plazapremiumlounge.com/en-uk/find/asia/indonesia/denpasar-dps-1/denpasar-dps/concordia-lounge-international-terminal) lists Wi-Fi and showers, subject to availability/possible charges. Priority Pass publishes 06:00–02:00 and this partner publishes 05:00–01:00; opening hours remain unknown with the conflict explained. Its generic food/beverage description is not promoted to a buffet or restaurant. Older reviews saying there are no showers do not overwrite current primary facilities.
- [Priority Pass Blue Sky Premier](https://www.prioritypass.com/en-GB/lounges/indonesia/ngurah-rai-international/dps11-blue-sky-premier-lounge) establishes domestic access near Gate 6, 05:30–21:30 hours and a three-hour visit. [Blue Sky's own Bali page](https://www.blue-sky.co.id/product/blue-sky-premier-lounge-bali) verifies buffet/live cooking, coffee, showers and Wi-Fi. Paid VIP rooms and uncertain beverage inclusions are disclosed without assuming all services are included.
- Garuda's official lounge and benefit pages returned HTTP 403. No Garuda cabin or alliance access rule has been inferred from a third-party review. A future Bali update should prioritize Garuda's airline lounge routes and current Concordia hours/quality evidence.

## Integration and regression cases

- Country coverage: add **VN → SGN, HAN** and **ID → DPS**; KIX is already mapped to JP. `NH`, `JL` and `VN` must be selectable operating carriers. No new membership IDs are required; native Lotusmiles/ANA/JAL tiers would widen future coverage.
- NH Business on an international KIX departure matches Kansai, not Premium. JL Premium Economy matches Premium; generic JL Economy does not. Other airlines must not inherit NH-specific admission.
- NODOKA remains landside/terminal-aware, conditional on the repeat-visit restriction, and unavailable on the modeled Priority Pass route more than twelve hours before departure. A midnight-crossing visit must not encounter a synthetic 23:59 closure.
- SGN ordinary Lotus T3 accepts VN domestic Business, not Priority Pass. SkyTeam Elite Plus domestic admission remains conditional on the missing connection evidence. Rose is an international-terminal alternative, not a domestic shortcut.
- HAN SH and DPS Blue Sky retain three-hour caps and terminal/route restrictions. Blue Sky must reject an international DPS departure. Concordia's unknown/conflicting hours must not produce a confident open/recommended state.
