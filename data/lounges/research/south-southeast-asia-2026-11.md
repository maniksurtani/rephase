# South and Southeast Asia hub batch — 18 November 2026

Checked on **18 November 2026, Australia/Brisbane**. This adds **11 selected lounges at six airports**: BKK 2, KUL 2, DEL 2, BOM 1, CGK 2 and MNL 2. These are initial international-departure guides, not complete inventories. Nine have editorial quality assessments; two intentionally remain unrated. Every record links to a checked review or the membership provider's photo/details page. No images were downloaded into the repository.

Operational facts come from opened airline/operator/program pages. Where a public page rendered only its shell, normal Chromium rendering or its public structured page data was used. Missing amenities remain unknown: an absent shower icon is not evidence of no showers. Access exceptions are encoded as conditions rather than hidden in notes. Quality scores are Rephase judgments from the identified firsthand review, not imported customer averages.

- [February 2026 Prestige review](https://liveandletsfly.com/thai-airways-royal-orchid-prestige-lounge-bangkok-review/) supplies the 4/5 editorial assessment and photo link. “Prestige” is the review's name; the current operator calls the D4 location Royal Orchid. The assessment excludes its First section.
- [Priority Pass BKK9](https://www.prioritypass.com/en-GB/lounges/thailand/suvarnabhumi/bkk9-miracle-business-class-lounge) confirms Miracle Business at D5, daily 00:00–23:59 and a two-hour stay. Public facility data confirms showers and Wi-Fi. Its unusual “children are not admitted” restriction is retained as a confirmation condition, not silently ignored.
- [January 2026 Miracle Business D review](https://pointstoseats.com/2026/01/03/lounge-review-the-miracle-business-class-lounge-at-bangkok-suvarnabhumi-bkk-international-concourse-d/) supplies the independent 4/5 assessment.
- Follow up: domestic A, Royal First, EVA/SilverKris, oneworld lounges and SAT-1. The E-concourse Royal Silk review is **not** applied to Royal Orchid E: the reviewer describes another lounge across the corridor. The adjacent Miracle First location likewise needs its own current access record.
