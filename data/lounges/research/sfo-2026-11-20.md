# SFO coverage additions — 20 November 2026

Added three distinct facilities: United Club F Gates Rotunda, Air Canada Maple Leaf Lounge, and the temporary Terminal 2 Centurion Lounge. The original five SFO records and their source dates are unchanged. The airport review date advances because these new records received operational research today. All links below were opened and read; searches were discovery only. No photographs were downloaded or embedded.

## Primary evidence

- [SFO lounge directory](https://www.flysfo.com/passengers/shop-dine-relax/lounges) identifies Maple Leaf in Terminal 2, open 05:30–19:15 daily, and Centurion in Terminal 2 Level 2, open 05:00–23:00. [Page 2](https://www.flysfo.com/passengers/shop-dine-relax/lounges?page=1) identifies United's F-gates rotunda on Level 3, open 05:00–midnight. Only this principal United Club is added, rather than proliferating near-equivalent Club entries.
- [Chase's United Club guide](https://www.chase.com/personal/credit-cards/education/rewards-benefits/guide-to-united-lounges) supports paid Club membership with a same-day United/Star Alliance boarding pass. United's own access URLs returned only a JavaScript shell, and the Star Alliance policy returned HTTP 403. Therefore no blanket premium-cabin or Star Gold entitlement was inferred. This conservative boundary matches the existing DEN/IAD records. The same-calendar-day requirement is an explicit condition. The premium-ticket and status routes remain a future operational research task, not a negative assertion that those passengers cannot enter.
- [Air Canada's access policy](https://www.aircanada.com/content/aircanada/ca/en/aco/home/fly/premium-services/maple-leaf-lounges.html) confirms Star Gold and qualifying premium-ticket access, a normal three-hour entry window and the connection exception. Star Gold on a Star-operated departure has no US-domestic exclusion at this Air Canada facility: United elite status can therefore match here without being misrepresented as United Club access. Premium-cabin-only matching is conservatively international, avoiding automatic US-domestic First eligibility. Aeroplan 50K/75K/Super Elite expand to Star Gold in the existing program model; Aeroplan 25K/35K do not.
- [Air Canada's SFO opening release](https://www.aircanada.com/media/air-canada-opens-first-maple-leaf-lounge-at-san-francisco-international-airport/) confirms the actual lounge's terrace, hot/cold buffet and showers. Its 2023 publication date is retained in the source title; today's check is not a claim of a new opening. Air Canada's general lounge policy also documents lounge Wi-Fi. A dedicated quiet area and spa remain unknown.
- [Virgin Australia's Air Canada policy](https://www.virginaustralia.com/jp/en/travel-info/at-the-airport/lounge/international/air-canada/) explicitly includes Velocity Platinum Plus alongside Platinum and Gold, but specifies Air Canada-marketed **and** operated international departures. That narrower policy is encoded for this route, even though Air Canada's general page uses broader operator wording and older tier names. No benefit is inferred on United or from Velocity Silver.
- [Amex's SFO facility page](https://www.americanexpress.com/en-us/travel/lounges/the-platinum-card/SFO/The-Centurion-Lounge---Temporary-Location-Terminal-2-Concourse-D-rUJ5NvGkPK/) confirms the temporary Concourse D location near Compass Books/Napa Farms Market, showers and Wi-Fi. The permanent Terminal 3 lounge is expected to reopen in 2027. Its hours rendered as `InvalidDateTime`, so airport-published hours are used. It confirms food without specifying a service format; structured dining stays unknown rather than importing a review's buffet description into operational facts.
- [Centurion access policy](https://www.americanexpress.com/en-us/travel/lounges/centurion-network/access/) and [full terms](https://www.americanexpress.com/en-us/travel/lounges/centurion-network/terms/) establish three-hour normal entry and five-hour same-day connection entry, with same-day round trips excluded from the connection exception. Platinum Companion/Employee Business Expense exclusions and same-day confirmed boarding passes remain explicit conditions. Reserve access is Delta-marketed **or** operated, represented as separate rules rather than requiring both. The full terms require payment with a US-issued Amex, and a qualifying US Reserve/Reserve Business card; those remain conditional because the app cannot establish payment or card subtype. Amex's short Reserve product marketing page says “booked with Reserve,” while the specific lounge terms permit a US-issued Amex; the lounge terms govern this record. Delta Sky Club Basic-fare exclusions were not copied into Centurion policy without supporting Centurion terms.

## Matching boundaries and regression cases

No new carrier, airport, membership or schema mappings are needed. AC and DL already exist. Worth checking in the shared tests:

- United Club membership on a United departure can match F Gates conditionally; Priority Pass, generic Star Gold, or domestic United First alone must not establish this limited membership-only rule.
- United 1K on domestic United can match Maple Leaf through its Star Gold tier; United Silver/Aeroplan 35K cannot. A Star Gold card with an AA-operated flight must fail, even if the marketing carrier is UA.
- Maple Leaf normal entry opens three hours before departure. Earlier access requires the explicit connection condition. Velocity Platinum Plus requires AC marketing and operation, not merely UA/Star operation.
- Centurion does not accept Priority Pass by itself. Delta Reserve must match when either the marketing **or** operating carrier is DL, remain conditional on card/payment facts, and fail when neither is DL. Platinum uses the ordinary three-hour rule or conditional five-hour connection rule; no rule grants a six-hour early window.
- SFO Centurion must be displayed as open in temporary Terminal 2, not closed merely because permanent Terminal 3 is being renovated.

Unsupported memberships remain unmodeled rather than mapped to the wrong card: Chase Sapphire Reserve/J.P. Morgan Reserve, eligible Aeroplan premium cards, paid Maple Leaf memberships and Centurion cards. Premium-ticket and Star-status United Club policy remain a follow-up research gap; normal guest entitlements are outside the present traveler-only matcher.
