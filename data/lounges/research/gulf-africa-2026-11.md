## Evidence and material boundaries

- **DOH:** [Qatar Garden](https://www.qatarairways.com/en-us/lounges/al-mourjan-garden.html), its [facilities announcement](https://www.qatarairways.com/press-releases/en-WW/225226-qatar-airways-welcomes-passengers-to-the-new-al-mourjan-business-lounge-the-garden/), the current [status-lounge matrix](https://www.qatarairways.com/en-sd/lounges/platinum-and-gold-lounges.html), [Gold South facilities](https://www.qatarairways.com/press-releases/en-WW/218271-qatar-airways-unveils-platinum-gold-and-silver-lounges-for-privilege-club-members-and-oneworld-partners/) and [JAL's Doha eligibility matrix](https://www.jal.co.jp/jp/en/inter/service/lounge/doh/index.html) establish separate premium-fare and status routes. Business Lite is conditional/excluded from complimentary Garden entry. No status-only Garden rule is added. Gold South has no inferred Platinum restaurant or quiet-room amenities. Current primary pages did not verify fixed hours for these two Qatar lounges, so hours remain unknown. [Priority Pass South Node](https://www.prioritypass.com/en-GB/lounges/qatar/hamad-international/doh2-al-maha-lounge-south-node) establishes its four-hour visits and busy-period restrictions; [DragonPass South Node](https://en.dragonpass.com.cn/lounge/102029) confirms snacks. North/Orchard and Southeast Al Maha locations are separate future records.
- **AUH:** [Etihad](https://www.etihad.com/en/plan/fly-with-etihad/lounges) and the [airport's Business lounge page](https://www.zayedinternationalairport.ae/en/shop-dine-and-experience/hotel-and-lounges/lounges/etihad-business-lounge) support current Terminal A coverage. The [Etihad Hub fare reminder](https://www.etihadhub.com/en-ae/news/reminder-business-value-fare-brand) explicitly excludes Business Value and identifies Comfort/Deluxe as eligible fares; that distinction is now a matching condition. Both First and Business pathways also require confirmation of the published 3.5-hour pre-departure access window and overnight transfers: the [manage-booking FAQs](https://www.etihad.com/en/help/faq/manage) do not guarantee overnight access, and the sources do not resolve exceptions for connecting premium passengers. The four-hour paid-pass limit is not imposed as a hard cap on complimentary premium tickets. [Pearl's airport page](https://www.zayedinternationalairport.ae/en/shop-dine-and-experience/hotel-and-lounges/lounges/pearl-lounge) and [Priority Pass](https://www.prioritypass.com/en-GB/lounges/united-arab-emirates/abu-dhabi-international/auh12-pearl-lounge) disagree on floor numbers and shower fees; the record uses Pier D directions and asks users to check their shower entitlement. The buffet is verified by the airport. Do not use reviews of the old terminals or copy historic Etihad/Velocity partnership access.
- **JNB:** [SAA's current directory](https://www.flysaa.com/en/manage-fly/before-flying/at-the-airport/lounges) separates International Premium from Platinum and Arrivals. It supports SAA Business, Star Gold and listed Velocity tiers on SAA. [Bidvest's operator page](https://www.bidvestlounge.co.za/commercial-lounge-or-tambo-international/) and [Priority Pass](https://www.prioritypass.com/en-GB/lounges/south-africa/o-r-tambo-international/joh-bidvest-premier-lounge) support the visit window, while Priority Pass explicitly warns of ongoing refurbishment with the lounge open. [Amex's location-specific directory](https://www.americanexpress.com/en-us/travel/lounges/the-platinum-card/JNB/Bidvest-Premier-Lounge-Terminal-A-voli-internazionali-VhT5lt4QvU/) verifies buffet service, not a new Amex admission rule. Bank-card and SAA Voyager-specific pathways remain gaps.
- **ADD:** [Ethiopian's hub directory](https://www.ethiopianairlines.com/us/services/services-at-the-airport/lounges-at-addis-ababa) is the operational source. Outbound Cloud Nine eligibility is distinct from Gold status; an inbound Business ticket alone does not qualify. The Platinum, Gold and Silver sections are not interchangeable. The five-hour entry window uses departure time, with delayed-flight confirmation in notes because the source mentions both scheduled and estimated departure. The Gold section does not inherit Cloud Nine's review score. No independent membership option was sufficiently verified for this pass.
- **NBO:** [Kenya Airways](https://www.kenya-airways.com/en-gb/experience/at-the-airport/lounge-services/) supplies Pride's facilities and eligibility; [DragonPass](https://en.dragonpass.com.cn/lounge/3941888) identifies T1A Gate 17 and showers. Pride's overnight hours remain unknown: the program lists 24 hours while the airline's general day-pass terms say 05:00–00:01 without clearly allocating that schedule to each lounge. [Delta's SkyTeam policy](https://www.delta.com/us/en/skymiles/medallion-program/international-partner-skyteam-benefits) supports international status access. [Aspire's operator page](https://www.aspirelounges.com/airport-lounges/nairobi-jomo-kenyatta-airport-aspire-lounge/) and [Priority Pass](https://www.prioritypass.com/en-GB/lounges/kenya/jomo-kenyatta-international/nbo3-aspire-nairobi) identify the separate T1B lounge and its three-hour visits. Paid access and individual contracted-airline invitations are not inferred from alliance status.
- **CMN:** The current [RAM lounge directory](https://www.royalairmaroc.com/int-en/royal-air-maroc-lounges) describes the renovated Terminal 1 lounge; its [status directory](https://www.royalairmaroc.com/ru-en/access-to-lounge-areas) names Le Zénith I, and [oneworld benefits](https://www.royalairmaroc.com/jo-en/oneworld/frequent-flyer-benefits) establishes Sapphire/Emerald pathways. Rules require confirming the assigned lounge and terminal access. Terminal 2 Zénith reviews and domestic Médina facilities are not merged into this T1 record. Exact hours and showers remain unverified. [Aspire T2](https://www.aspirelounges.com/airport-lounges/casablanca-international-airport-aspire-lounge-terminal-2/) and [Priority Pass](https://www.prioritypass.com/en-GB/lounges/morocco/casablanca-mohammed-v-intl/cmn7-aspire-executive-lounge-casablanca) agree on the A8 area but describe floor numbering differently; that ambiguity is disclosed. Its three-hour pre-departure window is encoded as such, not silently treated as unrestricted entry for a three-hour stay.
- **CAI:** [EgyptAir Alioth](https://www.egyptair.com/en/fly/lounges/Pages/ALIOTH_LOUNGE.aspx), the [German airline directory](https://www.egyptair.com/de/fly/lounges/Seiten/default.aspx), and [EVA's Star Gold policy](https://www.evaair.com/en-gb/infinity-mileagelands/about-infinity-mileagelands/tiers-and-privileges/benefit-when-flying-with-star-alliance-members-partners/Default.html) support the T3 airline/status record. The German airline page states premium/status eligibility that was absent from the current English directory's introductory text. [Priority Pass](https://www.prioritypass.com/en-GB/lounges/egypt/cairo-international/cai7-ahlein-premium-lounge) and [Aegean's Cairo directory](https://en.aegeanair.com/travel-info/travelling-with-aegean/at-the-airport/lounge-finder/?AirlineCode=A3&AirportCode=CAI&CabinClass=B&ShowMore=false) verify AHLEIN Premium T2, including paid showers and three-hour membership visits. AHLEIN Emerald T2 and Premium T3 are different facilities.

Priority Pass sometimes displays only its first five amenities in extracted page text. For DOH South, AUH Pearl, JNB Bidvest, NBO Aspire, CMN Aspire and CAI AHLEIN, the lounge's actual `facilities` objects embedded in its public HTML were also inspected. Only positive, location-specific flags were promoted to `yes`; false default flags do not establish an amenity's absence. Unknown dining types were not inferred from photographs or copied from reviews. Most independent lounge brands offer paid visits, but that is not automatically a free-access rule.

## Ratings and photographs

Thirteen records have independent editorial ratings with the exact articles linked in `qualityRating.sources` and `photoLinks`. Photos remain external page links; no image files are copied or embedded. CMN Aspire instead links to its checked operator gallery without inventing a rating.

Review limits are retained in the summaries: Gold South and Etihad use 2024 visits; SAA Premium uses two 2024 visits; Cairo Alioth's review dates to 2021 and should be refreshed; Pride's favourable 2026 account was only a 30-minute visit. Bidvest's current refurbishment may change its reviewed experience. The Al Maha review's obsolete “only Priority Pass lounge” statement is expressly not used as inventory evidence. Garden's 2026 review notes uneven service despite otherwise strong facilities.

Unrated: `add-star-gold` lacks a reliable firsthand account clearly tied to the present Gold section; `cmn-aspire-executive-t2` lacks a sufficiently detailed verified independent review. These should sort as unrated, not acquire a guessed or borrowed score.
