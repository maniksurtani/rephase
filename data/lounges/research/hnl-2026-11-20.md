# HNL lounge depth review — 20 November 2026

Added three records, taking HNL from four to seven lounges. The four existing records and their individual source dates remain unchanged.

## Facilities and primary evidence

- **ANA Lounge** and **ANA Suite Lounge** are separate facilities above C4 in Terminal 2, confirmed by the [airport directory](https://airports.hawaii.gov/hnl/flights/airport-lounges/). [ANA's HNL policy](https://www.ana.co.jp/en/jp/guide/lounge/international/hnl/) separates premium-cabin, Gold, paid-membership and ANA-specific rights. Suite has narrower access; generic Star Gold cannot open it. International-cabin rules deliberately exclude ordinary US domestic First. The ordinary lounge's Gold rule follows ANA's explicit Star-operated-flight criteria without inventing a marketing-carrier requirement.
- ANA's post-July 2026 opening is **08:50 until its last departure**: flight-dependent, not an invented fixed closing time. The [US operator page](https://www.ana.co.jp/en/us/travel-information/lounges/honolulu/) supports buffet service, Wi-Fi and the absence of showers. The Suite's small made-to-order selection is not represented as a full restaurant. Dedicated quiet rooms and spas remain unknown. Old search snippets mentioned a Wi-Fi outage, but fresh reads of the Japan-English, US and [GB pages](https://www.ana.co.jp/en/gb/travel-information/lounges/honolulu/) no longer contain that notice.
- **JAL Sakura Lounge / Admirals Club** is the central third-level facility above The Local, across from checkpoint 3. It is distinct from **Sakura Lounge Hale above C1**. [American's location page](https://www.aa.com/web/i18n/travelInformation/airportAmenities/hnl-club.html) and its [content-only version](https://www.aa.com/pubcontent/en_US/travelInformation/airportAmenities/hnl-club.html) both currently give **04:45–23:00**; stale 22:00 snippets were rejected. Amenities remain unknown because current primary pages do not specify them and secondary sources conflict on showers.
- [American's access policy](https://www.aa.com/web/i18n/travel-info/clubs/admirals-club-access.html) supports membership and status pathways. Separate marketing/operating alternatives avoid requiring both for an AA membership flight. Generic alliance status retains issuer restrictions. Domestic premium access is conditional on a ticketed Flagship seat on a named qualifying route; domestic First alone never becomes eligible. The policy's explicit HNL exclusion is for uniformed military access, not general Admirals membership.

## Quality and photographs

All three additions have independent exact-facility reviews and an editorial **3.5/5** rating; no new quality-backlog entries are needed.

- [LoungeReview's ANA Lounge report](https://loungereview.com/lounges/ana-lounge-hnl/) describes good space, service and views but ordinary catering and limited privacy; visit date is unspecified.
- [Live and Let's Fly's Suite review](https://liveandletsfly.com/ana-suite-lounge-honolulu-review/) is a **2019 visit, republished in 2023**, not current first-hand evidence. Its service strengths and limited dining/daylight are disclosed as historical.
- [Neil's May 2024 central Sakura review](https://neilsoneworld.wordpress.com/2024/05/16/hnl-terminal-2-jal-sakura-lounge-honolulu-airline-lounge-review/) describes seating, work desks and variable food service. It concerns the central joint club, not Hale.

Reviews inform ratings only; they do not refresh operational amenities or hours. Each photo link opens a review page. No images were copied, embedded or downloaded.

## Remaining scope and useful regression cases

- ANA Diamond, Million Miler lounge cards, Suite vouchers and JAL-specific status are not represented by current membership IDs. Do not substitute generic alliance status for Suite admission.
- United Club is conditional pending confirmation that the member's tier includes partner lounges; one-time passes are excluded. Advance-paid ANA entry has fare, ticket-stock and booking-deadline restrictions and is intentionally not encoded in this pass.
- JAL's current HNL details page describes **Hale**. Its Premium Economy / Flex Y rules were not copied to the central joint facility; confirm those rights separately. Membership rights for Aer Lingus and marketing-only non-AA oneworld itineraries are also outside this initial central-club subset.
- Regression targets: ANA Premium Economy grants the ordinary lounge, not Suite; Star Gold on United grants the ordinary lounge, not Suite; international Star First can match Suite; domestic United First alone cannot; Admirals membership on AA matches the central club but not Hale; Qantas Gold on a marketed-and-operated oneworld flight matches the central club; AA domestic First remains conditional without a confirmed Flagship route/product.
