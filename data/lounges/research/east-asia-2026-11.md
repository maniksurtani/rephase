# East Asia hub research — 18 November 2026

Research date: **2026-11-18 (Australia/Brisbane)**. This pass adds 20 lounge records at seven new airports (HKG, TPE, HND, NRT, PEK, PVG, CAN) and improves ICN with two renovated Korean Air lounges. ICN's existing Matina record and its 17 November evidence dates are preserved. These are useful initial guides, not complete airport inventories.

Official operator, airport and membership pages were opened and read for operational facts and access. Independent firsthand accounts were separately opened for editorial assessments. Ratings are Rephase's rubric-based judgments, not copied customer averages. Five new records remain unrated because a suitable independent firsthand account of the exact lounge was not verified. No images are copied or embedded: `photoLinks` point to the reviewed articles.

## HKG — Cathay premium tiers and a membership alternative

- The Pier Business and The Pier First are separate locations near Gates 65 and 63 respectively, both on level 6. Their midnight-crossing hours are 05:30–00:30.
- Business accepts qualifying oneworld premium cabins or Sapphire/Emerald; First requires First or Emerald. Both marketed and operating airlines must qualify. Cabin rules remain conditional on fare eligibility: Qatar Business Lite and Finnair Business Light exclusions are material. Complimentary Business-to-First upgrades do not grant The Pier First access.
- Cathay's current Business page says the **Bureau is temporarily closed** and the **Teahouse closed from 4 October 2026**. These facility closures are not treated as a whole-lounge closure. The Business quality review predates them and says so explicitly.
- Kyra here is **Gate 23**, not the newer Gate 40 lounge. Its current Priority Pass entry window is three hours before departure, maximum three-hour visit, and last entry/order is 23:30. Use the current membership hours rather than older review hours.
- Follow-up: Cathay Silver's carrier-specific entitlement, The Wing after reopening, Gate 40 Kyra, Qantas and other airline alternatives. Do not imply that a First lounge admission guarantees a spa appointment.

## TPE — do not merge EVA cabin and status lounges

- EVA's **The Infinity** serves eligible premium cabins; **The Star** serves Star Alliance Gold on Star departures. Both publish 04:30–23:30 and shower facilities. The Garden and The Club are not interchangeable with these lounges.
- A February 2026 firsthand review of both locations found crowding and average catering, with The Star offering more seating variety. Both receive a pragmatic 3/5 editorial score rather than inheriting the airline's onboard reputation.
- Oriental Club publishes 24-hour access with reduced food/seating 23:00–05:00; **showers unavailable 23:00–05:00** and buffet ends 22:30. Current maintenance closures begin at 23:00 on **11, 18 and 27 November 2026**, reopening at 05:00 the next day. Each closure is represented by exceptions for both affected calendar dates.
- Because the matcher has no amenity-specific operating schedule, Oriental's access includes an explicit condition to confirm the desired facilities for the visit. This prevents an unconditional overnight shower recommendation. **Follow-up: amenity schedules separate from entry hours**, so daytime users do not need the same conditional warning.
- China Airlines' official lounge page was opened but the current page exposes an airport-selection JavaScript shell without the actual TPE lounge details. No Dynasty location or access entitlement was invented. **CI Dynasty, Starlux, T1 and the remaining EVA tiers are explicit coverage gaps.**

## HND and NRT — terminal and lounge-tier boundaries

- HND includes ANA Terminal 2 International, JAL Sakura main lounge in Terminal 3, and TIAT Terminal 3. NRT includes ANA Satellite 5 in T1, Sakura main building in T2, and KoCoo in T2.
- ANA Lounge is separate from ANA Suite Lounge. ANA's official page explicitly grants regular lounge access to Star premium cabins, Star Gold, qualifying paid Star carrier club members and ANA Premium Economy. Do not apply ANA Premium Economy privileges to other airlines' Premium Economy.
- ANA and HND Sakura close according to departures, so hours are flight-dependent; no convenient fixed closing time was guessed. NRT Sakura publishes 07:30–22:00. NRT ANA's shower pre-booking rollout is postponed; its page instructs visitors to use an in-lounge kiosk.
- **TIAT operates 24 hours but Priority Pass entry is only 01:00–05:00.** Both the airport and membership provider confirm the limitation. The modeled hours intentionally reflect the access route supported in this guide. Old reviews claiming unrestricted all-day Priority Pass are used only for qualitative observations.
- JAL HND Premium Economy is represented with the airport-upgrade exclusion and JAL operating/marketing requirements. Other airlines' Premium Economy is not sufficient. Economy Flex and JAL programme-specific benefits need further modelling.
- NRT KoCoo is the airside lounge, not the landside IASS Executive Lounge. The current Priority Pass text does not expose the full facilities list, so unverified amenity fields remain unknown even though the independent review describes them.
- **Gaps:** ANA HND T3, JAL First and Sakura Sky View, Cathay HND, other NRT airline lounges and Japanese domestic lounges. NRT Sakura currently has no quality rating, rather than reusing the HND review. NRT airport lists Cathay's lounge as closed for renovation; it was not added as an available alternative.

## PEK, PVG and CAN — mainland source limits and conservative matching

- Air China's own membership directory confirms PEK Business in T3E east of the fountain and PVG Business No. 71 in T2 level 4. ANA's PEK/PVG pages explicitly describe eligible Star business and Star Gold users. First, domestic, transit and separate premium lounges must not be conflated.
- Air China's English lounge page exposes tab headings but not current detailed operational content. **PEK and PVG Air China hours and unconfirmed amenities remain unknown**; an old review's hours are not substituted for a current airline schedule. Star Alliance's general policy page returned 403, so it is not cited as a successfully checked source.
- A June 2024 PEK review describes an overcrowded overnight visit. Its historical Priority Pass access is deliberately **not** used as a current eligibility rule. Current BGS T3E Priority Pass membership provides a separate alternative near Gate E19. The separate T2 Air China/PP listing could not be verified in sufficient detail and is omitted.
- PVG No. 77 is verified through Priority Pass and Plaza Premium. Showers may cost extra; Priority Pass lists day beds and a two-hour limit. China Eastern branding does **not** establish general SkyTeam/premium-cabin access. That entitlement remains unmodelled until a current carrier policy for this exact lounge can be read.
- CAN domestic and international Sky Pearl lounges occupy different security zones. The international operator page explicitly identifies First/Business passengers and gives 04:30 until the last flight; CZ premium-cabin and PP routes are represented separately. **China Southern is not in SkyTeam.**
- CAN domestic hours conflict: the carrier says **05:45–21:00**, while Priority Pass says **04:30 until the last CZ flight**. The record uses unknown hours with both claims visible, rather than granting access during a disputed window. Domestic cabin entitlement remains a follow-up rather than inferred from the lounge's brand/name. Showers and sleeping rooms are supported by the carrier's facilities list. Massage chairs/areas do not establish a staffed spa.
- BGS T3E, Air China PVG No. 71, China Eastern PVG No. 77 and CAN domestic have no editorial score yet. A review of one of the airport's other numbered lounges is not evidence for these locations.
- **Mainland follow-ups:** Chinese-language confirmation of current hours, actual departure-terminal assignments after terminal changes, China Eastern/SkyPearl-specific fare and status rules, domestic/airline lounge coverage. The global membership programme selector does not yet include PhoenixMiles or SkyPearl tiers. Generic Star Gold covers verified alliance routes; no fictitious programme IDs were introduced.

## ICN — renovated Korean Air locations, not historical layouts

- Add **Prestige East Right** (August 2025 renovation) and **Prestige East Left** (January 2026 opening), both opposite Gate 253 on level 4 of T2, with airport-directory hours of 06:00–22:00.
- The Left opening announcement was read in Korean. It confirms the shower rooms, live food stations, cooking activities, arcade and ramyeon library; this is not evidence for a quiet sleeping area. The Right announcement confirms the wellness/relaxation facilities and showers.
- Eligibility uses Korean Air Prestige Class or SkyTeam Elite Plus on same-day international SkyTeam departures. First/Miler Club and limited-use Morning Calm benefits are separate tiers. Reviews mention temporary Asiana/Star access during integration, but **that transitional claim is not made an access rule without a current airline policy**.
- The independent reviews cover March/April 2026 visits to each newly renovated location. Their comments are not transferred to older West/Garden lounges. Existing Matina remains unchanged.
- **Gaps:** current West/Garden locations, exact Asiana transition entitlements, other SkyTeam cabin access, SKYPASS tiers and oneworld's ICN lounge.
