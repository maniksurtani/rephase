# Shenzhen (SZX) lounge research — 20 November 2026

Four selected lounges are represented in `SZX.json`, using the airport catalog's `Asia/Shanghai` timezone. This is a useful initial guide, not a complete inventory. All listed source pages were opened and read on the checked date; search snippets and directory star averages were not used as evidence for ratings. Photos remain external links to articles, with no copied or hotlinked images.

Domestic Hall 3, the international King lounge and the satellite are separate records. Hall 3 Gold access is conservatively limited to ZH/CA-operated domestic departures. No generic paid-Star-membership admission is inferred. PhoenixMiles-specific rules are omitted because the current membership contract does not represent them and operator/Air China pages disagree about Silver redemption; general Star Gold remains supported.

King opening hours remain flight-dependent: references to continuous snack service do not establish round-the-clock admission. Private rooms exist, but their availability and eligibility require confirmation; massage facilities do not imply a staffed spa. Internet workstations do not establish Wi-Fi, which remains unknown.

For International Guest, the live Priority Pass page excludes children while Amex allows under-twos. This conflict is disclosed in `note`, not an adult admission condition: the matcher evaluates the traveler, not accompanying children. Its meal format remains unknown rather than describing a suspended buffet as available. For the satellite, contradictory primary partner schedules make hours unknown. Food is documented, but the type is not. Do not infer China Southern or SkyTeam premium/status entitlement merely from the lounge name.

## Integration and follow-up

The parent verified [ANA's Shenzhen Airlines partner page](https://www.ana.co.jp/en/jp/guide/plan/codeshare/zh/) and [Miles & More's Shenzhen Airlines page](https://www.miles-and-more.com/row/en/program/partners/shenzhen-airlines.html) on 20 November 2026 for the `ZH` airline selector and Star Alliance mapping. Useful policy checks: United 1K/Star Gold with ZH domestic should match Hall 3 but not the international King lounge; Star-operated international Business should match King International; Priority Pass international must not match the domestic satellite; the satellite visit must cap at 120 minutes and retain uncertain opening hours. A non-Star economy flight should not qualify for King merely because the traveler has Star Gold.
